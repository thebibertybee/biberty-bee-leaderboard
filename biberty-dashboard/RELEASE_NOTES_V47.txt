THE BIBERTY BEE — ANALYTICS DASHBOARD VERSION 47
Accuracy, comparisons, reports, and quality assurance
November 22, 2026

WHAT CHANGED

1) ACCURACY AND DATA QUALITY
- Observed Interaction Rate now uses the average of likes + comments on the SAME posts where both counters are present; no longer adds unrelated likes and comments averages.
- Interaction sample sizes are shown independently of likes-only and comments-only sample sizes. Missing metrics are never considered zero. Legitimate recorded zeroes remain zero.
- Strict account matching prefers Instagram user IDs, then exact handles/declared former usernames, then unique exact display-name matches. Conflicting known IDs are not silently joined. An exact handle match is permitted when the source has an ID but the registry does not.
- Post feeds use their own latest successful snapshot date as the 30-day anchor, separately from the weekly summary timestamp and follower readings. Coverage mismatches are compared only when source timestamps are sufficiently close. Potentially outdated account-level snapshots are flagged.
- A successfully downloaded follower feed is no longer labeled unequivocally "Up to date." The page identifies recent, older, or unverified readings; post updates show separate source dates.
- Asynchronous refreshes are sequence-checked, so a slower older response cannot overwrite a newer request.
- Post summary calculations index records once per calculation and report snapshots are cached per preview for better performance with large historical feeds.

2) COMPARISONS
- Generated detailed reports now open with an executive summary covering audience size, aligned follower-growth observations, eligible typical likes, audience-adjusted Like Rate, and caveats.
- Where follower readings do not overlap closely, the report declines to name a synchronized growth winner rather than comparing mismatched time windows.
- Content tables show paired interaction sample sizes and explain why current-follower-normalized rates are not reach, views, or publication-time interactions.

3) REPORTS AND PDF EXPORT
- A new editorial executive summary uses the existing navy/brick/white Biberty brand style.
- Corrected report terminology for observed interaction rates, eligible samples, and unknown coverage.
- Standalone print documents include a base URL for images and styles; page-break rules are refined for long charts, appendices, and readable tables.
- Original navy-gradient cover and Georgia serif headings retained. The optional full post appendix remains OFF by default.

4) QUALITY AND EXPERIENCE
- Improved mobile input and table behavior, focus outlines for keyboard navigation, and older-data status styling.
- Code and browser-smoke tests completed against controlled synthetic fixtures (not live Instagram data): desktop, 390px mobile, report preview, standalone PDF export, 21-post deduplication, and a 25,000-post math calculation.

DEPLOYMENT
Upload the CONTENTS of biberty-dashboard/ to your existing GitHub Pages folder at:
  biberty-bee-leaderboard/biberty-dashboard/
Keep the folder name/path unchanged so existing Wix embeds keep working.
Do not publish the synthetic QA test fixtures (not included in this ZIP).

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- GitHub Pages and Wix could not be tested against their live loaded Google Sheets/Instagram traffic in this environment. Complete the checks in QA_CHECKLIST_V47.txt after deploying.
- No new permissions or Instagram Insights access are introduced. Private reach, views, impressions, saves, and shares remain unavailable.
- Older posts' likes are latest recorded values, NOT likes collected during their year of publication.
- The current-follower denominator is not a historical-follower denominator. Like and interaction rates are not view rates.
- Eligibility of five posts is a ranking minimum, NOT evidence of statistically complete coverage.
- Some Instagram external links may still be blocked by Wix iframe or popup settings and require published-site testing.
- PDF appearance may depend on browser print settings; enable background graphics for accurate brand colors.
- Version 46 is unchanged and should be retained as a rollback option.
